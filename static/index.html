A NOTE ON SOURCES & CONSENT

Good matchmaking starts with good boundaries.

The 25 built-in personas are fictional test data, not real people or scraped profiles. To add a real adult, use their explicit permission, their two profile URLs, and a self-written summary they approved. This demo stores those URLs as references; it does not scrape Instagram or LinkedIn, infer sensitive traits, send messages, or arrange real dates.
